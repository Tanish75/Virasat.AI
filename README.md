# 🕊️ Virasat.AI (विरासत) — Compassionate Inheritance & Claim Assistant for Bharat

> **Empowering Indian families to navigate legal paperwork, bank settlements, and inheritance rights with dignity and zero middlemen.**

---

## 🎯 The Real Bharat Problem
When an Indian family loses a loved one, the grief is compounded by a complex, bureaucratic nightmare:
- **Banking hurdles:** Banks often illegally demand costly Succession Certificates even when nominees are registered.
- **Scattered legal procedures:** Every state has different portals (UP eDistrict, Aaple Sarkar, etc.).
- **Middlemen exploitation:** Grieving families end up spending ₹20,000–₹50,000 on agents just to get their own rightful money.

## 💡 What Virasat.AI Does
- 📋 **State-Wise Guided Dossier:** Maps requirements based on your exact state and asset type (Bank, LIC, Property, Pension).
- ⚖️ **RBI Mandate Guard:** Informs citizens about their rights (e.g. RBI circular mandating 15-day settlement for nominee claims without court orders).
- 📝 **Auto-Drafted Formal Applications:** Ready-to-print legal claim letters customized with deceased & nominee details.
- 📥 **One-Click Download:** Instant offline document generation.
